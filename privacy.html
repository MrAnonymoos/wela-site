Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what the Wela website (wela.pro) collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our login provider, not in plain text.
  • Plan info: your plan (free, Pro, or Unlimited), subscription status, and renewal date.
  • Billing info: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, but we never see or store your full card number.
  • Technical info: basic data your browser sends (such as IP address and browser type), used by our providers for security and to run the site.

We do not ask for your Polymarket private keys or trading credentials through this website.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, provide your plan and features, process subscriptions, send account emails (such as confirmation and password reset), prevent abuse and fake signups, and answer support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: account database and login.
  • Stripe: payment processing and subscription management.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on our login and signup forms.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.

These providers process data to run their services and have their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and local storage

Your browser stores a login session so you stay signed in. Our bot-protection provider may also set technical cookies. We do not use advertising trackers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time from your account page (cancel any active subscription first). This removes your account and plan record from our database. Stripe may keep payment records as required by tax, accounting, and legal rules.

Security

We use reputable providers and access controls to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

Age

Wela is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact
